**Real Map Implementation of Greedy Best-First** 🚀

Ever wondered how algorithms navigate complex spaces? Here's a visualization of the Greedy Best-First search algorithm in action, navigating the intricate streets of Downtown Dubai! At its core, Greedy Best-First is an informed search algorithm that prioritizes expanding nodes that appear to be closest to the goal. It utilizes a heuristic function ($h(n)$) to estimate the cost from the current node ($n$) to the goal. Unlike A* search, it *only* considers this heuristic, effectively ignoring the cost incurred to reach the current node. This means it greedily "runs" towards the target based on the heuristic's prediction. To manage the exploration, it typically uses a **priority queue**. Each element in the priority queue is ordered by its heuristic value, ensuring that the node with the smallest estimated cost to the goal is always explored next. This makes it remarkably fast for finding *a* path, but it's not guaranteed to be the *optimal* (shortest) path due to its short-sighted nature.

Our "Real Map Implementation" transforms the urban landscape of Downtown Dubai into a digital graph. Each **intersection** represents a **node**, and the **streets connecting them** are the **edges**. The red and green lines you see trace the **visited edges** as the algorithm explores potential routes, demonstrating its expansion process. The bright **yellow line** showcases the **computed path** it ultimately finds to the destination. Implementing this on real-world road networks presents unique challenges compared to simple grid-based environments. Road networks are often non-uniform, with varying edge weights (travel times, distances, traffic conditions) and complex connectivity. While a grid might have uniform edge costs, real roads can have speed limits, one-way systems, and turn restrictions, all of which would be modeled as edge attributes in a more sophisticated graph representation. This makes the heuristic's accuracy and the algorithm's efficiency on such dynamic and irregular structures crucial.
