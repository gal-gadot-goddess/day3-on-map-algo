**Real Map Implementation of Bidirectional Dijkstra 📍🗺️**

Ever wondered how navigation apps find the *absolute fastest* route between two points? Today, we're diving deep into the **Bidirectional Dijkstra algorithm**, the ultimate optimizer for shortest path calculations. Unlike traditional Dijkstra which expands outwards from a single source, Bidirectional Dijkstra cleverly initiates *two simultaneous searches*: one from your starting point (A) and another from your destination (B). Each search uses a priority queue to explore nodes, always prioritizing those with the lowest cumulative path cost from their respective origins. This strategy dramatically prunes the search space, as the two searches meet somewhere in the middle, significantly reducing the total number of nodes and edges that need to be examined. The algorithm terminates when the frontiers of both searches intersect, guaranteeing the shortest path with unparalleled efficiency.
