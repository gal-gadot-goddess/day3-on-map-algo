## Real Map Implementation of Bidirectional A* 🗺️

Ever wonder how navigation apps find the fastest route through a bustling city? Meet Bidirectional A*, the speed king of pathfinding algorithms! Unlike a standard A* search that typically radiates outwards from a single start point, Bidirectional A* intelligently deploys two A* searches simultaneously: one originating from the start node and the other from the goal node. These "smart searches" race towards each other, dramatically reducing the search space.

The core of A* and its bidirectional variant lies in its heuristic-guided exploration. At each step, the algorithm prioritizes exploring neighboring nodes based on a cost function: `f(n) = g(n) + h(n)`.
