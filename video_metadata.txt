**Real Map Implementation of Dijkstra**

Ever wondered how your GPS finds the quickest route? This visualization dives deep into Dijkstra's Algorithm, a cornerstone of shortest path finding. At its core, Dijkstra systematically explores a graph, prioritizing nodes based on their accumulated distance from a starting point. It uses a **priority queue** to efficiently select the unvisited node with the smallest known distance. When a node is selected, its neighbors are examined. If a path through the current node to a neighbor offers a shorter distance than previously recorded, that neighbor's distance and predecessor are updated, and it's re-prioritized in the queue. This iterative process continues until the destination node is reached, guaranteeing the **exact shortest path** by weighing every "road" (edge) with its associated "travel time" or "distance" (weight).
