**Real Map Implementation of Bidirectional Dijkstra**

Ever wondered how GPS apps find the *fastest* route, not just the shortest? Dive into the mechanics of Bidirectional Dijkstra's Algorithm! Unlike a standard Dijkstra that expands outwards from a single source, our bidirectional approach launches *two* searches simultaneously: one from the starting point and another from the destination. Each search expands outwards, prioritizing neighbors based on their *cumulative path cost* from their respective origins. We achieve this efficient prioritization using a **priority queue** for each search, which always returns the node with the lowest accumulated distance. As these two search frontiers expand and eventually meet, they form a "meeting point." The shortest path is then found by summing the distances from the source to the meeting point and from the destination to the meeting point. This simultaneous exploration significantly reduces the search space, especially in large graphs, leading to a dramatic performance improvement over a unidirectional search.

Our visualization brings this powerful algorithm to life on a real-world map of Singapore's Downtown Core. Here, we've transformed the intricate network of streets and intersections into a graph structure. Each **intersection** acts as a **node**, and the **streets** connecting them are the **edges**. These edges are assigned weights representing the travel time or distance. In the video, the vibrant red and green lines vividly illustrate the "visited edges" by each of the two Dijkstra searches as they explore their respective halves of the graph. The crucial yellow line? That's the computed shortest path, revealed once the two search frontiers successfully meet. While a grid-like graph can be optimized well, real-world road networks present a unique challenge with their non-uniform edge weights, complex intersections, and potential for one-way streets. Bidirectional Dijkstra is particularly adept at navigating these complexities, offering a guaranteed shortest path with remarkable efficiency.
